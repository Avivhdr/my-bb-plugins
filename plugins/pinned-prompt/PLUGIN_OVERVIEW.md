Keeps your last message pinned at the top of the chat while you read a long reply.

## What you get

- A bar at the top of each thread showing your newest message above the view.
- Click it to jump back to the full message.
- It hides while any of your messages is on screen. Scroll up past a message and the bar switches to the one before it.

## How it works

The plugin reads the thread's messages from the bb server on this machine and draws the bar over the chat. Nothing leaves the machine. It has no settings.
